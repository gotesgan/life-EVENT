Frequently Asked Questions
What is VenueLook?

VenueLook is an online-to-offline marketplace for event venue and vendor discovery and booking. As with any great team, VenueLook is a good mix of sharp, intelligent, focused, hard-working team. We're young, we're enthusiastic and yes, we love hunting down the coolest venues around.

How do I get started with VenueLook?

You can get started by registering your event venue/vendor business. Registration is completely Free and takes just 5-10 minutes. In order to register, click on the GET STARTED button on this page.
You can choose a premium package or list for free. In premium profiling, you will be assigned a Relationship Manager who you can chat with/talk to in case of any concern. If everything is okay, your profile would be approved within 24 hours & you would start getting customer enquiries on your mobile phone, email and on your web dashboard.
In case you need further information, reach out to us at sales@venuelook.com

How does the approval process work?

Clients come to VenueLook to choose venues and vendors whom they can trust with special occasions of their life. To ensure that, after you fill up your business details with us, we check the veracity of the information provided by you. In case of discrepancy or non availability of information, we would ask you to provide more information. After that, your profile will be made live on the platform.

What are the charges for my online profile?

Basic Listing of your business on VenueLook is Absolutely Free. For Premium listings, there are subscription packages you can choose from.

How is it FREE? Is there a catch or bluff?

Let us assure you, there is no catch. Our aim is to create an ecosystem for Event planning which is trusted by all Stakeholders - Event Hosts and Service providers.

In which all categories can I create my VenueLook profile?

You can create profile in one or more of the following categories for wedding and non-wedding events:
- Venue
- Caterer
- Photographer/Videographer
- Makeup Artist
- Decorator/Florist
- Mehndi Artist
- Wedding Invitation
- Choreographer
- DJ
- Jeweller
- Wedding Planner

We'll be adding more categories soon! :)

I don’t have a website. Can I create my VenueLook profile?

Yes, you can do it. You don’t necessarily need a website. Your Facebook/Instagram page should be enough in establishing credibility with potential customers.

I already have enough business. Why should I go online on VenueLook?

70% of the clients in major cities go online to decide about at least one of their event services. Building a strong brand online, starting with VenueLook would ensure you don't miss out on your digital presence and attracting customers who are searching online.

Can I get business from outside India on VenueLook?

15-20% of our clients are from outside India. NRI’s who are planning their wedding in India can contact you directly via your profile on VenueLook.
