Frequently Asked Questions
Do Banquet hall prices include decoration cost?

Most wedding halls in Delhi provide inhouse decor & their pricing includes decoration cost as well. Make sure that you check with your venue what all is included in the package and what are the overhead charges.

Can we customize decorations in banquets in Delhi NCR? Can we hire our own decorator?

Many banquet halls provide basic decor when a booking is made. In most cases, the venue has their own inhouse panel of wedding decorator in Delhi NCR that you can choose from and customise your decor. Some will allow you to hire your own decorator. You can check on their decor policy.

What is the catering policy in Delhi NCR banquets? Can I hire our own caterers?

Many Delhi banquets have their in-house caterers who serve delicious food at various events hosted at the venue and you can ask for food tasting. Some however can allow you to bring your own wedding caterer in Delhi from outside.

Does every banquet provide inhouse DJ?

Not all banquet halls in Delhi NCR provide an in-house DJ for the events. Check with your wedding venue if they provide this facility, otherwise you can easily find DJ’s for weddings in Delhi NCR on WMG.

What capacity should I look for in banquets in Delhi NCR?

Before you start looking for a Delhi banquet hall, you will first have to finalise the guestlist for the wedding ceremony and then find and book a venue allowing that capacity accordingly.

How do Banquet halls in Delhi NCR charge?

Most marriage halls in Delhi charge on a per plate basis wherein they charge depending upon the number of plates used by the guests. The cost usually includes food, decor & other facilities. Some venues would charge on a Rental basis wherein the banquet halls charge only for the space that they provide and all other services are provided by a third party.

Can we get alcohol from outside?

Different venues have different policies, some marriage halls in Delhi NCR do have licence & offer inhouse bar or some will allow their clients to bring in their own alcohol whereas others have strict no alcohol policy.

Are there rooms available in Banquets?

Many banquet halls provide complimentary rooms for the couple and their family to get ready and stay in till the wedding ceremony. Other than this, some wedding hotels in Delhi NCR also have rooms available so that all your outstation guests can stay at the same place.

What are the cancellation policies?

All banquet halls have different cancellation policies - some marriage halls in Delhi NCR charge a minimal cancellation fee. Check with the venue for their cancellation policy before booking.
